Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

This app is a private Home Assistant installation used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, it reads Google Calendar events so they can be shown on a display in the home.

How the data is used

  • Calendar data is stored only on the household's own Home Assistant server.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • It is not used to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
